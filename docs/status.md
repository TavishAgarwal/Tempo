## Findings worth telling the team
- With a TomTom-calibrated (flat) Delhi profile, time-dependent planning barely beats static planning in the simulator. The first-round QPSO tied random restart because its step (α 1.0 → 0.5) turned every particle into a near-random tour (1–5% of pbest edges kept) that VND rebuilt from scratch; with the tuned step (α 0.05 → 0.01) QPSO beats PSO and random restart, ties GA up to n = 100, and still loses to GA at n ≥ 200 and to PyVRP at n ≥ 100. Per-particle attractor, rank-space update and a VND gate were tried in the tuning grid and lost. The other value of the system is the Split + VND + write-back pipeline, the incident response (safe plan in well under a second, warm re-optimisation) and the simulator-based evaluation.
- PyVRP (static matrix at departure slot, scored by our simulator) beats TA-QPSO at n ≥ 100, and GA beats it at n ≥ 200. Do not claim otherwise (rules.md).
- Everything on the TomTom side is network-wide; road-class sensitivities are still assumptions.
